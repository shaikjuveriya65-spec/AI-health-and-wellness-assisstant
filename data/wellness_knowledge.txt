Sleep: Most adults benefit from a regular sleep schedule and enough sleep to feel rested. A consistent bedtime and wake time, a comfortable dark room, less late-night screen use, and avoiding heavy meals close to bedtime can support better sleep. Persistent sleep problems should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

Hydration: Water needs vary with body size, activity, weather, diet, and health conditions. Drinking water regularly and paying attention to thirst and urine color can help with normal hydration. During heavy exercise or very hot weather, fluid needs can increase.

Physical activity: A balanced routine can include walking or other aerobic activity, strength exercises, mobility work, and rest. Beginners should increase activity gradually and choose activities they can perform safely and consistently.

Healthy eating: A balanced eating pattern can include vegetables, fruits, whole grains, pulses or other protein foods, healthy fats, and appropriate portions. Limiting highly processed foods and excessive added sugar can support overall health.

Protein foods: Protein can come from eggs, milk or curd, fish, chicken, beans, lentils, chickpeas, soy foods, nuts, and seeds. Needs vary by age, body size, activity level, and health status.

Stress management: Useful general strategies can include slow breathing, regular physical activity, sufficient sleep, time management, social connection, hobbies, and short breaks. If stress is persistent or seriously affecting daily life, professional support can be helpful.

Healthy habits: Small habits that are repeated consistently are usually easier to maintain than extreme short-term changes. Setting one or two realistic goals at a time can make lifestyle changes easier.

Smoking and alcohol: Avoiding tobacco supports health. Alcohol can have health risks, and less alcohol is generally lower risk than more. People should follow local health guidance and avoid alcohol when it creates safety or health problems.

Safety: The assistant should not diagnose a medical condition from symptoms. Chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, sudden weakness or confusion, heavy bleeding, loss of consciousness, or other potentially life-threatening symptoms require urgent medical attention.
